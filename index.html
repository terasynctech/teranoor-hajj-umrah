FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get an Umrah package price?

Send your departure city, travel month, number of pilgrims and preferred hotel category. We will prepare an enquiry based on current availability and rates.

Can I request a family or group package?

Yes. Use the custom journey form and mention your group size, room requirements and any special needs.

Are the prices shown on the website final?

No. Package prices can change with flights, hotel availability, dates, room types and other services. Final pricing should be confirmed before booking.

Can you arrange hotels in Makkah and Madinah?

You can enquire about available hotel options. The final hotel depends on availability, dates, room category and the services included in your package.

Do you provide Hajj packages?

Hajj enquiries can be submitted through the website. Any Hajj service or authorization claim should only be made when the required arrangements and permissions are in place.

How can I contact TeraNoor?

Use the WhatsApp, phone or enquiry form. The website currently routes enquiries to +91 76338 01161.
