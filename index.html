FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Some common questions before planning your Hajj or Umrah.

Do you arrange Umrah visas?

We can guide you on the required travel and visa process and the documents needed for your package.

Are flights included in the package?

Flights can be included or arranged separately depending on the package and your selected travel dates.

Can I choose my Makkah and Madinah hotels?

Yes. Hotel options can be discussed based on location, category, dates, budget and availability.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Airport pickup, drop and local transportation can be arranged according to your itinerary.

Can families travel together?

Yes. Family and group arrangements can be planned around common travel dates and accommodation needs.

Can you arrange Ziyarat?

Yes. Ziyarat arrangements can be included as part of the travel plan, subject to itinerary and availability.
